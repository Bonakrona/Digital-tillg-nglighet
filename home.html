This is a website made for an assignment in the course Digital accessibility, TSNS05, at Lunds Technical University.
It is specifically made to help you reach your full reading potential. This is done by customizing the page according to your specific needs. With practice, you will get more comfortable with reading and increase your pace. Good luck!


Configure the settings according to your preferences. The settings are pre set to an appealing appearence. Therefore you can choose to leave the setting as it is. If you want to try out different options you can do so with each of the settings. When you are satisfied click start. This will you be moved to another page.

You will recieve three different options, Moving option, One word option and Mark option.
Moving option: It allows you to see five words at a time, where the point is to read the highlighted word positioned in the middle.
One word option: Shows one word at a time.
Mark option: It shows the whole text, and the reader is supposed to follow the highlighted area.
